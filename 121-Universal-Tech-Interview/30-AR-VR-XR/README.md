# 30 - AR / VR / XR

> Augmented, virtual, and extended reality — building immersive spatial experiences.

## What It Covers

- **Core**: AR, VR, MR, XR, Spatial Computing, HMDs, Controllers, Tracking
- **Rendering**: 3D Graphics, Unity/Unreal for XR, Real-Time Rendering, FOV
- **Practice**: Hand/Eye Tracking, SLAM-based tracking, Spatial Anchors, Optimization (latency, frame rate)
- **Applications**: Games, Training, Architecture, Healthcare

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is the difference between AR, VR, and MR?
2. How does inside-out tracking work?
3. How do you prevent motion sickness in VR?
4. How do you place virtual objects in the real world (AR anchors)?
5. What are the performance constraints of XR apps?
6. How do you handle interaction in VR (controllers, hands, gaze)?
7. Explain a pass-through AR camera pipeline.

## Prep Tips

- Build one AR/VR scene in Unity with XR Interaction Toolkit or similar.
- Know tracking fundamentals: IMU, camera, SLAM, and calibration.
- Understand the 60fps requirement and frame timing in XR.
