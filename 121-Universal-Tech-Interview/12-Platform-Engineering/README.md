# 12 - Platform Engineering

> Internal developer platforms (IDPs) that turn infrastructure into self-service product.

## What It Covers

- **Core**: Internal Developer Platforms (IDP), Golden Paths, Developer Experience
- **Self-Service**: Environment provisioning, service catalog, CLI/portal
- **Tech**: Backstage, ArgoCD, Crossplane, Terraform, Kubernetes platforms
- **Ops**: Platform teams, SRE collaboration, governance, standardization

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is an Internal Developer Platform? Why build one?
2. What is a "golden path" and how do you design it?
3. How do you balance platform standardization vs developer freedom?
4. What is the difference between platform engineering and DevOps?
5. How would you design a self-service environment provisioning flow?
6. What are the KPIs for an internal platform?

## Prep Tips

- Understand the journey: DevOps → SRE → Platform Engineering.
- Be ready to design an IDP: portal, catalog, templates, pipelines, guardrails.
- Know Backstage and how it wires developer portals.
