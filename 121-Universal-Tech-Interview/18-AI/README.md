# 18 - AI

> The broad field of artificial intelligence: reasoning, learning, and intelligent systems.

## What It Covers

- **Core**: AI fundamentals, Knowledge Representation, Reasoning, Search (BFS/DFS, A*, Minimax)
- **Fields**: ML, DL, NLP, CV, RL, Expert Systems, Planning
- **Modern**: GenAI, LLMs, AI Agents, Multimodal AI, AI Safety/Ethics

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is the difference between AI, ML, and DL?
2. Explain a* search and when to use it.
3. What is supervised vs unsupervised vs reinforcement learning?
4. Explain the concept of an AI agent (perceive → reason → act).
5. What are the current limits of AI systems?
6. What is the Turing test? Is it still relevant?
7. How would you build an AI system for a specific business problem?

## Prep Tips

- Distinguish classic AI (search/reasoning) from statistical ML/DL.
- Be able to explain search algorithms and game trees (minimax).
- Connect AI concepts to the roles you're applying for.
