# 36 - Interview Preparation

> The master guide for interview rounds, strategies, and the mock interview system.

## What It Covers

- **Rounds**: Resume Screen, HR/Phone, Technical (DSA, System Design, Machine Coding), Behavioral, HR/Final, Offer
- **Skills**: Communication, STAR Method, Problem-Solving, Mock Interviews, Timing, Question Types
- **Strategy**: Company Research, Question Prediction, Weak Area Analysis, Feedback Loops, Confidence
- **Tools**: The Mock Interview system (START, NEXT, HINT, ANSWER, EXPLAIN, HARDER, EASIER, WEAK AREAS, RETEST, FINAL ASSESSMENT)

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is the typical interview process for a tech company?
2. How do you answer a question you don't know?
3. How do you structure a system design answer?
4. Walk me through your project in 2 minutes.
5. How do you handle pressure in an interview?
6. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
7. How do you explain a technical concept to a non-technical person?

## Prep Tips

- Run the mock interview system daily with the Interviewer Agent for every domain.
- Track weak areas in a tracker and retest them weekly.
- Rehearse your projects, behavioral stories, and system design structure aloud.
