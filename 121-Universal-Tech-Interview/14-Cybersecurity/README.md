# 14 - Cybersecurity

> Protecting systems: application security, network security, cloud security, and identity.

## What It Covers

- **Core**: CIA Triad, Threats, Vulnerabilities, Risk
- **App Security**: OWASP Top 10, Injection, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, Authentication & Authorization
- **Network**: Firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPN, Zero Trust, TLS, Encryption, Hashing
- **Cloud/Identity**: IAM, MFA, SSO, Secrets, Incident Response
- **Practice**: Penetration Testing, Secure Coding, Compliance (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR)

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What are the OWASP Top 10? Explain the top 3.
2. How do you prevent SQL injection and XSS?
3. Authentication vs authorization — give a real example.
4. Explain TLS handshake and why HTTPS matters.
5. What is Zero Trust architecture?
6. A user's account was compromised — walk through your response.
7. How do you securely store passwords?

## Prep Tips

- Memorize OWASP Top 10 with attack + defense for each.
- Practice secure coding in your projects (parameterized queries, input validation).
- Know incident response phases: prepare, identify, contain, eradicate, recover, lessons.
