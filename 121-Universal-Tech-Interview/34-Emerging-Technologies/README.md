# 34 - Emerging Technologies

> The technologies reshaping the industry — and how to interview about them credibly.

## What It Covers

- **GenAI**: LLMs, Agents, RAG, Multimodal, GenAI in products
- **Web3**: Blockchain, DeFi, DAOs
- **Others**: Edge Computing, Quantum Computing, Spatial Computing (AR/VR), Biotechnology (Bioinformatics), Low-Code/No-Code, Cybersecurity (Zero Trust)

## Sample Interview Questions

1. Which emerging technology are you most excited about and why?
2. How would you evaluate a new technology for production adoption?
3. What are the practical limits of current GenAI systems?
4. How do you keep up with rapidly changing technologies?
5. What is the difference between hype and real adoption?
6. How would an LLM change how you design a system?

## Prep Tips

- Have 2-3 informed opinions with concrete examples (papers, products, results).
- Show a learning system: sources you follow, experiments you run.
- Connect emerging tech to your target role to show relevance.
