# 31 - Enterprise Technology

> SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, ServiceNow, and the enterprise software ecosystem.

## What It Covers

- **ERP**: SAP (FICO, MM, SD, ABAP), Oracle ERP/DB
- **CRM**: Salesforce (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Apex, Lightning), Dynamics 365
- **ITSM**: ServiceNow (ITSM, CMDB, Service Portal)
- **Core**: Enterprise Architecture, Integrations (APIs, Middleware), Data Migration, Business Process

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is an ERP and how does it integrate business processes?
2. What is the difference between SAP, Salesforce, and ServiceNow?
3. What is a CRM and how is it different from an ERP?
4. How do you integrate an enterprise system with other apps?
5. What is a CMDB in ServiceNow?
6. What is Apex in Salesforce and when do you write it?
7. How do you migrate data from a legacy system to Salesforce?

## Prep Tips

- Pick one ecosystem (SAP, Salesforce, or ServiceNow) and know it deeply.
- Understand the architecture: business process → system → data → integration.
- Be ready to talk about a business process flow, not just features.
