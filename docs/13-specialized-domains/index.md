## How to Use These Guides

1. **Identify your primary domain** (and one adjacent fallback domain).
2. **Learn the fundamentals** — every interview starts with basics, even for senior roles.
3. **Build a domain project** — a demo board, RTL block, or ML model beats theory.
4. **Practice the question bank** per domain.
5. **Mock-interview the domain** with a peer or mentor.

## Common Threads Across All Domains

- **Know your projects end-to-end** — architecture, decisions, trade-offs, numbers.
- **Fundamentals beat trivia** — interviewers probe depth, not memorization.
- **Bring tools** — FPGA board, PCB design, Jupyter notebooks, or a drone build log are portfolio gold.
- **Domain + software skills** (Python, Git, Linux, version control) are the strongest combination.
