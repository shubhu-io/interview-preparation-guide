## Smart Portal Tactics

- Set **email/SMS job alerts** for your target keywords on every portal.
- Keep profiles **100% complete** — incomplete profiles rank lower in recruiter searches.
- Refresh your profile every few days (Naukri "profile update" banner, LinkedIn "Open to work") — recruiters prioritize recently-active candidates.
- Upload a clean, ATS-friendly resume on every portal (see [Resume & ATS](resume-ats)).
- Apply on the company career page first, then mirror on portals.
